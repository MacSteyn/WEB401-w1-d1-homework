California's Greatest Cliffs

The greatest cliffs are these four cliffs located in southern California. Each cliff has its own uniqueness and beauty: West Cliff, North Cliff, East Cliff, and South Cliff are the pride of California. They offer recreational areas, camping sites, wildlife watch spots, and overnight cabins.

No matter which direction you head from Irvine, California, the sites are easily reached within a 100 mile radius. West Cliff is the most popular among Westcliff University students due to its proximity.
